THE GOAT

Privacy

Last updated November 27, 2026

The GOAT has no accounts, no ads, no cookies and no third-party trackers. We never sell your data.

You play under a random, anonymous player ID that the game makes on your device. We don't ask for your name, email or anything else about you.

Kept on your device

These stay in your browser's local storage and are never sent to us on their own:

  • Your anonymous player ID
  • Your referral code, used in links you share
  • Your game progress for the day
  • Your stats: streak, games played and win rate
  • The dates of your first and latest visit, used only for the visit count below

Clearing your browser's site data removes all of it and gives you a new player ID.

Sent to us

Each of these is stored with your anonymous player ID:

  • Picks and votes: who you think is the GOAT in each category.
  • Suggestions: categories you suggest. They stay private until we review them.
  • Game events: visits, games started and finished, shares and similar actions, so we can see what people enjoy and fix what breaks. We don't record anything you type except suggestions.

IP addresses are used only to stop spam and abuse through rate limits. We store a salted, one-way hash of your IP address, never the address itself.

Visit counts: when you open the site, we count the visit by approximate city, the site that sent you, and phone or computer. We don't keep your internet address. Your browser sends one short message to our own stats server (proctorpilot.com) saying which page you opened, the site you came from and whether it's your first visit, or your first that day. The server works out phone or computer from your browser's details and the approximate city from your internet address, then keeps only daily totals, with no player ID.

A nightly job deletes data as soon as it reaches these limits.

Who handles it

Game data is stored by Supabase, our database provider, in the United States. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, those services see your IP address when your browser requests a page or font. Visit totals are kept on our own server at proctorpilot.com (hosted by Cloudways, behind Cloudflare), which looks up the approximate city itself, in DB-IP's free city database, so your address isn't sent anywhere else for it.

How the rankings work

Each category starts from our own estimated ranking. As real votes come in, the crowd's votes count for more and more, until the ranking is driven by the community. Each ranking shows which stage it's at.

Content and corrections

  • Suggested categories are private until we approve them. Offensive content is removed right away.
  • Debates about private individuals aren't allowed.
  • If a puzzle has a factual error, we fix the category. A daily puzzle that's already out is never changed; instead, a correction note is shown under it.

Delete your data or contact us

Email info@intellismartstaffing.com to have your data deleted, to report a mistake, or with any privacy question.

For deletion, include your player ID, shown in the "Your data" note at the bottom of the game page. Because the ID is anonymous, it's the only way we can find your records.
